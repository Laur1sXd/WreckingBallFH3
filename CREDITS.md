WreckingBall FH3 development port

- Original WreckingBall 1.3.3: Trinculo54 and contributors,
  https://github.com/Trinculo54/WreckingBall
  Source inspected at commit 5deedbc423c8cda775f73411aa32ed19fc163292.
  Procedural smashable injection and spatial collision filtering are adapted
  from that project's GPL source. The FH2 DLL is not redistributed here.
- ExtraOptions: RokkuDayo and contributors,
  https://github.com/RokkuDayo/ForzaHorizon2_ExtraOptions
  The supplied FH3 0.4.4 binaries were inspected to determine the FH3 plugin ABI.
- FH3 Mod Platform by Levilente, based on Trinculo54's FH2 Mod Platform.
  The FH3 SDK header and its MIT license accompany the source.
- MinHook: Tsuda Kageyu and contributors,
  https://github.com/TsudaKageyu/minhook
  Source used by this project and its BSD-style license are in
  [third_party/minhook](third_party/minhook).

FH3 addresses and data layouts were determined from the user's local
unmodified Xbox One executable (SHA-256
e6a362a2fde118577ab3018c8e042e62a3bb42f83a63ace41145c0864527a661).
No game executable, model, texture, or other game asset is redistributed.

This port was authored with Codex. It is unofficial and is not affiliated
with Playground Games, Turn 10, Microsoft, or the Azimuth developers.
